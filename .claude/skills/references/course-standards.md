# LOVING Course Standards

## LMS: Absorb LMS

### Course Naming Convention
[DEPT]-[LEVEL]-[TOPIC] — e.g., FLD-101-HardscapeInstall, OFC-201-SalesforceBasics

### Module Naming Convention
[CourseCode]-M[#]-[ShortTopic] — e.g., FLD-101-M1-SitePrep

### Completion Criteria
- All lessons: viewed (100% progress)
- All quizzes: passed at 80% minimum
- Certificate: auto-issued on course completion

### Audience Levels
- Field Crew (bilingual EN/ES required)
- Crew Lead (bilingual preferred)
- Field Manager (English primary)
- Office Staff (English)
- Leadership (English)

### Assessment Standards
- Module quiz: 3-5 questions, 80% pass rate, 2 attempts before lockout
- Final assessment: 10+ questions drawn from randomized bank of 30+, 80% pass
- Remediation path: failed → review flagged modules → reassess

### Video Standards
- MP4 format, 1080p minimum
- Closed captions required (SRT file uploaded alongside video)
- Spanish audio track or SRT required for any Field or Crew Lead course
- Max 10 minutes per video segment (shorter for field crew: 5 min max)

### Job Aid Standards
- PDF format, single page preferred (two pages max)
- Font minimum 11pt for print legibility
- Laminated-ready format for field use
- Bilingual (EN/ES) for any field crew job aid
