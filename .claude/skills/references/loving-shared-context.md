## NC/SC Climate and Plant Context

Charlotte and the Piedmont: Zone 7b/8a. Hot humid summers, mild winters. First frost Nov-Dec.
Upstate SC (Greenville/Columbia): Zone 7b-8a, slightly warmer winters.
Triad: Zone 7a/7b, slightly cooler than Charlotte.
Asheville: Zone 6b/7a, cooler temperatures, earlier frost.

**Common plant material for NC/SC:**
- Grasses: Zoysia, Bermuda, Tall Fescue, Centipede (SC), St. Augustine (coastal SC)
- Shrubs: Boxwood, Holly, Loropetalum, Nandina, Knockout Roses, Encore Azaleas, Hydrangeas
- Trees: Crape Myrtle, Japanese Maple, Oak, Dogwood, Magnolia, Leyland Cypress, Arborvitae
- Perennials: Black-Eyed Susan, Salvia, Coneflower, Daylily, Ornamental Grasses
- Annuals: Marigold, Petunia, Lantana, Vinca, Caladium (shade)

**Seasonal relevance:**
- Spring (March-May): Primary selling and install season. Biggest revenue window.
- Summer (June-Aug): Irrigation high demand. Sod installs slow due to heat stress risk.
- Fall (Sept-Nov): Second planting season. Hardscape prime time (cooler temps for crews).
- Winter (Dec-Feb): Planning season. Quote pipeline builds. Hardscape continues in mild weather.

## Brand Voice for LOVING / UMB

**Tone:** Warm, expert, confident, and approachable. Never corporate. Never salesy.
**LOVING speaks like a knowledgeable neighbor who also happens to be the best landscaper in town.**
- Uses "your backyard," "your home," "your family" — personal, not generic
- Specific about what we install and how: names materials, plant varieties, techniques
- Honest about process: "Here's what to expect from start to finish"
- Proud of NC/SC market knowledge — we know this climate, these soils, these plants
- No generic filler phrases. No "we are committed to excellence." Say what you actually do.

**UMB voice:** Slightly more aspirational. Design-forward. Uses words like: transform, envision,
create, outdoor living, gather, entertain, escape. Still grounded in real project specifics.
