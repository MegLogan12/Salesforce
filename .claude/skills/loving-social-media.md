## Platform Specialists

### Instagram Specialist (@upgrademybackyard / @lovingcompanies)
**Algorithm priority:** Reels (highest reach), Carousels (highest saves and shares), Static (lowest).
**Audience:** Homeowners 28-55, aspirational, visually driven. They follow for inspiration and project ideas.
**Content pillars for LOVING/UMB:**
1. **Project Reveals** — before/after transformations. Single reel or carousel. Most powerful content.
2. **Process Content** — "How we built this patio in 3 days." Short-form documentary.
3. **Plant and Material Education** — "Why we use Zoysia over Bermuda in Charlotte." Teaches trust.
4. **Seasonal Tips** — "When to plant in NC" or "How to prepare your irrigation for winter."
5. **Behind the Scenes** — crew at work, morning coffee before a big install, equipment in action.
6. **Customer Moments** — reactions, testimonials (with permission), families enjoying the space.

**Caption formula:** Hook (first line stops the scroll) + Story (2-4 sentences) + CTA (one clear ask).
**Hashtag strategy:** 5-10 tags, mix of broad (#hardscape #outdoorliving), local (#charlottelandscaping
#triadnc #greenvillesc), and niche (#paverinstallation #zoysiasod #outdoorkitchen).
**Posting cadence:** 4-5x per week. Reels at least 3x per week. Peak times: 8-9am and 7-9pm EST.

**Content templates:**
- Project Reveal Reel script: [Before shot 2s] + [Demo/process 8-12s] + [After reveal 3s] + [Call to action overlay]
- Carousel: Slide 1 = hook photo + bold text, Slides 2-6 = process or detail shots, Last slide = CTA
- Story sequence: Poll (would you choose X or Y?) → Result reveal → Project show

### TikTok Specialist (@upgrademybackyard or @lovingcompanies)
**Algorithm priority:** Watch time and completion rate above all else. First 3 seconds must hook.
**Audience:** Homeowners 25-45, DIY-curious, entertainment-first. They want to be entertained and accidentally educated.
**LOVING content that works on TikTok:**
1. **Transformation Time-lapses** — entire backyard build compressed to 30-60 seconds with trending audio.
2. **"Did you know?" facts** — "Did you know most landscapers charge you double for delivery and don't tell you?" Controversy drives views.
3. **Crew Day POV** — first-person perspective of a day on the job. Authentic, real, no polish needed.
4. **Before/After Reveals** — "Rate this transformation" format. Ask for engagement explicitly.
5. **Myth-busting** — "You don't need to water new sod every day. Here's the truth." Educational hook.
6. **Responding to Comments** — stitch or duet format, answering real homeowner questions.
7. **Process Satisfying Videos** — paver laying, sod rolling, mulch spreading. The internet loves satisfying content.

**Caption formula:** Keep it short. 1-2 sentences max. Let the video do the work. CTA: "Follow for more" or "Comment your city."
**Hashtag strategy:** 3-5 tags. #outdoorliving #backyardtransformation #hardscape + one local (#charlotteNC).
**Posting cadence:** 5-7x per week minimum to feed the algorithm. Repurpose Instagram Reels with fresh captions.
**Audio:** Use trending sounds for reach. Use original audio when educational (algorithms value both differently).

**TikTok-specific hooks (use in first 3 seconds):**
- "I can't believe how much this backyard changed in 4 days..."
- "POV: You're the crew lead on a $85,000 patio install in Charlotte"
- "This is why your landscaper is losing you money on plant material"
- "Rate this transformation: before vs. after paver patio"

### Facebook Specialist (LOVING Companies / Upgrademybackyard)
**Algorithm priority:** Groups and local reach. Facebook is a community platform, not an entertainment platform.
**Audience:** Homeowners 35-65. Local community members. Neighborhood group participants.
**LOVING content strategy for Facebook:**
1. **Neighborhood Group Engagement** — share completed projects in Charlotte-area neighborhood groups
   (Ballantyne Moms, South Charlotte Community, Fort Mill Neighbors — with page disclosure per Meta rules)
2. **Project Galleries** — photo albums with multiple before/after shots and detailed descriptions
3. **Promotions and Seasonal Offers** — Facebook responds well to clear, direct promotional content
4. **Customer Reviews** — share 5-star Google reviews as post content. Social proof on a trust platform.
5. **Educational Long-Form** — "Everything you need to know before you get a patio quote" performs well on FB
6. **Local Events** — home shows, builder community events, seasonal planting workshops

**Caption formula:** More text than Instagram is fine on Facebook. Tell the full story. Add price ranges
("projects like this typically run $8,000-$15,000 in the Charlotte market") — homeowners appreciate transparency.
**Posting cadence:** 3-4x per week. Wednesday and Thursday evenings and Saturday mornings perform well locally.
**Facebook Ads:** Facebook remains the best ROI for local homeowner lead gen. Combine organic content with
paid boosting of best-performing posts.

### LinkedIn Specialist (LOVING Companies)
**Audience:** Builder account contacts (VPs, Purchasing Managers), commercial developers, subcontractors,
potential partners, commercial property managers, local business community, talent prospects.
**LOVING content strategy for LinkedIn:**
1. **Builder Relationship Content** — "LOVING recently completed our 500th DR Horton community installation
   in the Charlotte market." Credibility-driven.
2. **Leadership Perspective** — Megan's voice: operational insights, industry observations, company milestones.
   Personal posts outperform company page posts dramatically.
3. **Hiring Content** — Open positions, crew recognition, team spotlights. LinkedIn is a hiring channel.
4. **Industry Education** — "What landscape contractors wish builders knew about plant material lead times."
   Thought leadership that builder contacts will share.
5. **Company Milestones** — Years in business, markets entered, volume achieved.
6. **Partnership Announcements** — Salesforce implementation, UMB launch, new market entry.

**Caption formula:** Hook (bold statement or question) + 3-5 short paragraphs + CTA. Use line breaks liberally.
LinkedIn rewards longer posts that keep users reading.
**Posting cadence:** Company page 2-3x per week. Megan's personal page 3-4x per week for maximum reach.
**Hashtags:** 3-5 max. #landscaping #hardscape #outdoorliving #charlottenc #buildingrelationships

## Content Production Workflow

1. **Shoot:** Field Manager or Crew Lead captures job photos/video on phone before and after every install
2. **Collect:** Photos submitted via FSL Mobile photo completion gates → auto-tagged by job type
3. **Select:** Social team (or Megan) picks 2-3 best shots per job
4. **Create:** Caption drafted by loving-social-media agent, humanized, scheduled
5. **Schedule:** Buffer, Later, or Meta Business Suite for scheduling
6. **Engage:** Respond to every comment within 24 hours during business hours
