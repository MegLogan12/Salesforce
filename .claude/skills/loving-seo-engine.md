## The SEO Team

### 1. Local SEO and Google Business Profile Specialist
Local search is LOVING's highest-ROI SEO channel. Homeowners search "patio installer near me"
or "landscaping company Charlotte NC" and hire from the Google Local Pack.

**GBP Management for LOVING:**
- Separate GBP listings for each market (Charlotte, Triad, Greenville/Columbia, Asheville)
- Primary category: Landscaping Service or Landscape Architect
- Secondary categories: Paving Contractor, Irrigation Service, Sod Supplier (as appropriate per location)
- NAP consistency: Name, Address, Phone must match exactly across all directories
- Photos: update minimum 4 new project photos per week per location
- Reviews: respond to every review within 24 hours. 4.5+ star average is minimum competitive threshold.
- Google Posts: 1-2 posts per week per location (seasonal offers, project reveals, tips)
- Q&A: seed 10-15 common questions with LOVING-written answers

**Local citation strategy (must be consistent):**
- Primary: Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Facebook Business
- Industry: Houzz, Angi (formerly Angie's List), HomeAdvisor, Thumbtack, LawnStarter
- Local: Charlotte Chamber, Triad Business Journal directory, local community sites

### 3. On-Page and Technical SEO Specialist
Audits and optimizes every page on UMB and LOVING's web properties:

**On-Page Checklist (every page):**
- Title tag: [Primary Keyword] | [City] | Upgrademybackyard.com (under 60 chars)
- Meta description: benefit-driven, includes keyword and city, 140-155 chars, includes CTA
- H1: one per page, includes primary keyword
- Body copy: 300+ words minimum for service pages, 800+ for blog posts
- Internal links: 3-5 per page, linking to related service pages and blog content
- Image alt text: descriptive, includes keyword where natural
- Schema markup: LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage, Review, BreadcrumbList

**Technical SEO priorities:**
- Core Web Vitals: LCP <2.5s, FID <100ms, CLS <0.1 (Vercel hosting helps significantly)
- Mobile-first indexing: site must be fully mobile-optimized
- Crawl budget: ensure Google can discover all service and location pages
- Canonical tags: prevent duplicate content across similar service pages
- XML sitemap: submitted to Google Search Console, updated automatically

**URL structure:**
- /services/hardscape/patio-installation/
- /services/landscaping/
- /services/sod-installation/
- /services/irrigation/
- /locations/charlotte-nc/
- /locations/greensboro-nc/
- /locations/greenville-sc/
- /blog/ (content hub)
- /projects/ (gallery)
